# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Movie fans, subscribers, and content creators looking for a premium streaming selection and features.

## Product Purpose
Landing page showcasing CINEFLIX's premium streaming catalog and interactive features (Movie Search Simulator, Mockup Switcher, FAQ Accordion, Pricing) with production-ready quality.

## Brand Personality
Cinematic, premium, dramatic (rich dark backgrounds, high-contrast typography, crimson red accents).

## Anti-references
- Overused purple/cyan AI gradients
- Bounce-easing bounce-in animations
- Giant cards with over-rounded (>16px) corners
- Layout thrashing on width/height changes

## Design Principles
- Cinematic Dramatic Contrast: Solid premium crimson/white typography against dark backdrops.
- Precise Motion Physics: Clean exponential curves instead of standard bouncy physics.
- Structured Spacing: Rhythm-driven spacing without relying on rounded borders or stripes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast ratio >= 4.5:1 for body and large text.
- Reduced motion support via `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`.
