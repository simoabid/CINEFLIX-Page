FAQ

Got Questions?

Everything you need to know about CINEFLIX.

Is CINEFLIX really free?

Yes! CINEFLIX offers a free plan with access to the full TMDB library in 720p. No credit card required. You can upgrade to Standard or Family for 4K, offline downloads, and ad-free streaming.

Does it work on iOS?

CINEFLIX is currently Android-only. An iOS version is on the roadmap — follow the GitHub repo for updates.

Is it legal to stream content?

CINEFLIX uses the TMDB API for metadata and discovery. Actual streaming sources are publicly available. We comply with all TMDB attribution requirements.

How do offline downloads work?

Standard and Family plan users can download movies and episodes for offline viewing. Downloads are stored locally on your device and available for 30 days.

What devices are supported?

CINEFLIX requires Android 8.0 or higher. It's optimized for phones and tablets. The app is just 45MB and runs smoothly on mid-range devices.

How can I report a bug or request a feature?

Open an issue on the GitHub repository or reach out on X/Twitter. We respond to all reports within 48 hours.
